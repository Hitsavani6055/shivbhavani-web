Our Tradition

About Shivbhavani

Established in 2016 in Bhavnagar, Shivbhavani Safa Paghdi and Wedding Accessories Palace brings regal charm and heritage craftsmanship to your most memorable celebrations. We specialize in handcrafted groom turbans, matching baraati styling, and luxury ceremonial accents designed for a flawless royal fit.

What We Offer

Royal Safas & Paghdis: Jodhpuri, Rajasthani, Rajputi, and classic Gujarati styles in rich, breathable fabrics.

Groom Accessories: Statement kalgis, royal malas, ceremonial swords, dupattas, and stoles.

Barati Styling: Bulk color-coordinated safa packages with expert on-site tying services.

Buy or Rent: Flexible purchase options and rental packages to suit every budget.

Our Promise
